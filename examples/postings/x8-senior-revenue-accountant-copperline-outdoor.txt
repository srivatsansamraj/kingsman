Senior Accountant, Revenue
Copperline Outdoor Co.
Salt Lake City, UT (Hybrid)

About Copperline
Copperline Outdoor Co. designs and sells camping, climbing and trail gear through our own website, 22 retail stores and more than 300 wholesale partners. We are a fast-growing, privately held company with about 850 employees. Visit copperline-outdoor.example.com.

The opportunity
We are looking for a Senior Accountant to own revenue accounting across our direct-to-consumer, retail and wholesale channels. You will run the revenue portion of the monthly close, keep our revenue recognition policies current, and support our annual audit. You will report to the Assistant Controller and work closely with sales operations, e-commerce and FP&A.

Responsibilities
- Own the revenue close each month: journal entries, accruals for returns and wholesale allowances, and account reconciliations.
- Apply ASC 606 to new contract types and sales programs, and document the accounting conclusions.
- Reconcile e-commerce, point-of-sale and payment processor data to the general ledger.
- Prepare revenue flux analyses and explain variances to the Controller and FP&A.
- Maintain and test internal controls over revenue in support of our SOX readiness work.
- Prepare schedules and support for the annual external audit.
- Improve close processes and reporting in NetSuite.

Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in accounting or finance.
- CPA license, or actively working toward it.
- 4+ years of accounting experience, including revenue accounting; public accounting experience is a plus.
- Strong knowledge of US GAAP and ASC 606.
- Experience with NetSuite or another ERP system.
- Advanced Excel skills.
- Strong attention to detail and the ability to meet close deadlines.

Nice to have
- Experience with retail or consumer products companies.
- Experience with wholesale allowance and returns reserve accounting.
- Experience implementing or testing internal controls.

Compensation and benefits
The salary range for this role is $88,000 - $105,000 per year, plus an annual bonus. We offer medical, dental and vision insurance, a 401(k) with match, a generous gear discount, and paid volunteer days.

Copperline Outdoor Co. is an equal opportunity employer.
