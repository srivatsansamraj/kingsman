Offensive Security Engineer, Vulnerability Research
Blackfen Labs
Remote (United States)

About us
Blackfen Labs is a security research firm that finds and helps fix vulnerabilities in the software our clients ship: embedded device firmware, desktop applications, file parsers and network services. Our work ranges from two-week assessments to long-running research programs, and we publish what we can at blackfen-labs.example.com/research.

The role
We are looking for an Offensive Security Engineer to join our vulnerability research team. You will fuzz, reverse engineer and exploit software written mostly in C and C++, write clear reports for client engineering teams, and help them verify their fixes. New team members are paired with a senior researcher for their first two engagements.

Responsibilities
- Build fuzzing harnesses for parsers and network services using libFuzzer, AFL++ or custom tooling, and keep them running at scale.
- Triage crashes, identify root causes and determine exploitability.
- Develop proof-of-concept exploits for memory corruption bugs (heap overflows, use-after-free, type confusion) to demonstrate impact.
- Reverse engineer closed-source binaries with Ghidra or IDA to find attack surface.
- Test web and API front ends of client products when they are in scope.
- Write findings reports with reproduction steps, severity and remediation advice, and present them to client engineers.
- Contribute tools and techniques back to the team's internal research platform.

Qualifications
- 2+ years of experience in vulnerability research, penetration testing or exploit development, or equivalent research (CTF competitions, published CVEs, open-source security work).
- Strong C programming skills and a working understanding of memory safety, the stack and the heap.
- Experience with at least one coverage-guided fuzzer and with sanitizers such as AddressSanitizer.
- Experience with a debugger (GDB, WinDbg or LLDB) and a disassembler.
- Proficiency in Python for tooling and automation.
- Clear written English; our reports go straight to client engineering teams.

Nice to have
- Exploitation experience on modern mitigations (ASLR, DEP, stack canaries, CFI).
- Experience with Linux or Windows kernel internals.
- OSCP, OSED or equivalent certifications.
- A history of reporting vulnerabilities upstream and seeing them fixed.

What we offer
- Base salary of $130,000 - $160,000, plus a research bonus for published findings
- Fully remote team with two in-person research weeks each year
- Dedicated research time: 20% of every sprint is yours
- Health, dental and vision insurance; 401(k) match; home-office stipend

Blackfen Labs is an equal opportunity employer.
